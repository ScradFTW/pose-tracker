How it works

The skeleton isn't from a third-party pose library. It's a small convolutional net (~700k parameters) that I trained from scratch on the Leeds Sports Pose dataset (Johnson & Everingham, 2010; 2,000 labeled images, 14 joints) — no pretrained backbone, no transfer learning. It regresses a heatmap per joint; each webcam frame is resized to 256×256, run through the net as ONNX in WebAssembly, and the per-joint peak becomes a keypoint.

It's honest about its limits: single person, no hands or face, and noticeably rougher than commercial pose libraries trained on datasets orders of magnitude larger. Stand centered, mostly full-body, for the best results.
