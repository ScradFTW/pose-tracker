Pose-tracker: from-scratch ResNet18-style heatmap CNN on COCO keypoints
========================================================================

Dataset: COCO 2017 person_keypoints -- 124,823 train / 5,647 val instances
Note: PCK@0.2 here is normalized by the crop box size (a simplified
proxy), not official COCO OKS -- not directly comparable to papers.
Model: ResNet18-shaped backbone (random init) + 3-stage deconv head
Model params: 15,376,721
Epochs planned: 35 (cosine LR schedule, T_max=35)
Epochs actually run: 8 -- stopped manually once gains clearly plateaued
  relative to the ~9 min/epoch cost; the checkpoint below is the best-val
  snapshot, saved automatically whenever val PCK improved.
Training time: ~69 min on NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU
Best val PCK@0.2 (bbox-normalized): 0.932 (epoch 8)

For comparison, the earlier LSP-trained model (train/dataset.py,
train/model.py, 705,774 params, 2,000 images) reached val PCK@0.2 = 0.476
after the full 150-epoch run and had clearly started overfitting (train
PCK 0.90 vs val PCK 0.44 by the end). COCO's ~70x larger instance count
kept train/val PCK close together throughout (no overfitting gap observed
in 8 epochs), suggesting there was still headroom left in the unused 27
epochs of the cosine schedule -- this was a deliberate time/quality
tradeoff, not a quality ceiling.
